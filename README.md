# Marie's Hair & Beauty

Static marketing + booking-request site for Marie's Hair & Beauty (48 Victoria Street, Wolverhampton WV1 3PJ).

- Pages are generated by `python3 tools/build.py` (HTML is committed, no build step needed on deploy).
- `style.css`, `site.js` (shared effects + WebGL hero), `book.js` (booking), `gallery.js` (lightbox).
- Bookings and forms open a pre-filled email to the salon (no backend).
